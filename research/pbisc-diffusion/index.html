How to read the film

Two views of the same generation process.

Left / actual noisy state / PCA

What the sampler holds.

Each colored point is one generated cell’s current diffusion state, xₜ, with value and support channels. A fixed PCA, fitted on canonically encoded observed cells over 2,980 common measured genes, shows the actual state without clipping the remaining noise.

Right / predicted clean expression / UMAP

What the model expects.

At each step, the denoiser predicts a clean state, x̂₀(t). Its hard-gated expression output passes through normalization, PCA, and UMAP fitted only on observed real cells. This panel shows how the current estimate of the completed profile changes.

Keep your eye on the same points.

Colors carry final frozen scANVI predictions backward: T cell, B cell, Monocyte, NK cell, DC, and Other. These are transferred labels, not observed identities at intermediate steps. Other collects AIFI labels outside the five named groups; the benchmark confidence threshold is zero, so low-confidence predictions are retained. Gray points are 4,362 observed cells from the same donor whose cells supplied the condition. This comparison is not independent validation.

Second recording / SLE − HC

Follow the model-derived
expression contrast.

This recording uses a separate cohort of 1,500 generated cells per donor: 63,000 cells from 30 SLE and 12 HC donor conditions. It follows donor-level expression contrasts during denoising with fixed final predicted cell labels. Purple highlights 61 measured genes from the prespecified Hallmark interferon-alpha-response set; gray denotes other tested genes.

Horizontal axis

SLE–HC effect

Log₂ ratio of mean donor CPM, with a +1 pseudocount in each group. Positive values indicate higher generated expression in SLE.

Vertical axis

Frame-specific evidence

−log₁₀ of BH-adjusted q-values. The correction covers all six cell groups and 2,980 common genes within each frame, not significance appearing anywhere in the film.

Purple points

A fixed gene set

Membership in HALLMARK_INTERFERON_ALPHA_RESPONSE is set before analysis. Of its 97 members, 61 are in the common measured panel. Highlighting alone establishes neither actual exposure nor pathway activation.

The endpoint is the primary comparison.

Intermediate frames are exploratory. Cell membership and donor eligibility stay fixed; each eligible donor/type needs at least 20 endpoint cells, with at least five donors per group. Unsupported panels remain explicit. Welch tests use donor aggregates and do not include uncertainty from the generator, checkpoint, or transferred labels.

Recorded states, without motion interpolation

Both projections are fitted only on observed cells from the same conditioning donor. For the actual-state PCA, observed counts are encoded with the checkpoint’s value scaler and support rule. Expression UMAP uses common-panel normalization to 10,000, log1p, reference scaling, and PCA. Generated cells use the same fixed transforms through the final frame; axes remain fixed. The two panels use different coordinate systems.

Early predictions can lie outside the observed reference distribution; UMAP still places them near reference neighbors. Apparent clusters or trajectories can therefore be projection effects. These are computational denoising steps, not cell differentiation or biological time. Final labels come from one seeded Poisson readout for frozen annotation; projection and expression analysis retain continuous generated expression.
