One cell, step by step.

The model starts from pure noise and removes a little of it at each of 50 steps. At every step it also predicts the finished cell. The prediction picks a cell type within a few steps. The noisy state catches up much later.

Methods & data

  • Recorded run: PBISC-Diffusion ctx2048 E12 (EMA weights), DDIM with 50 steps and no added noise, one pseudobulk condition from GSE135779, generated cells. This page runs no new model inference.
  • Shown cells: for each type, the first generated cell whose final scANVI label is that type. The rule was fixed before looking at any result. The Monocyte is the same cell as on the main page.
  • Gene blocks come from the real cells of the same donor. A marker is at least 0.5 log1p(CP10k) above the next type and detected in at least 20% of its type. IFN-α = the measured genes of MSigDB HALLMARK_INTERFERON_ALPHA_RESPONSE. Shared = detected in at least half the cells of every type.
  • Gauge: marker genes only. 0 = the average of the five type averages, 1 = a typical real cell of that type. It agrees with scANVI on of real cells and of generated endpoints.
  • The noisy state is the decoded diffusion state. Its early steps are not biological counts. All values are log1p(CP10k) on the 2,980-gene panel, stored to 1/255 of the colour range.
