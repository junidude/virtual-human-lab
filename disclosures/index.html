What is public, what stays private.

VHL publishes enough information for readers to understand its research, people, governance, manuscript status, and scientific standards. It keeps signatures, private minutes, personal records, accounts, protected research material, and institutional approval records private.

Affiliation notice

Affiliations identify people. They do not create institutional sponsorship, endorsement, responsibility, or resource support unless a written agreement says so.

A public summary, with protected records withheld.

Private records are not published on this website or accepted through public contact channels.

Public

Research and organization

Research scope, manuscript status, consenting member and advisor names, professional affiliations, governance structure, representative role, and public standards.

Public when approved

Project-specific disclosures

Support, conflicts, data provenance, code or model access, licenses, and corrections are disclosed when relevant and approved for public release.

Private

Personal and administrative records

Signed bylaws, full minutes, individual votes, private contact information, identity documents, member registers, accounts, receipts, and approval letters.

Private

Protected research material

Controlled-access data, non-public datasets, patient information, security material, unpublished institutional or company information, and confidential agreements.

Not public yet

Full manuscripts under review

During patent filing and peer review, full manuscript PDFs are available only by private email request. The website displays blurred previews.

Never submit by email

Patient, access, and security data

Do not send patient information, credentials, secrets, controlled-access data, non-public military or government information, or confidential material through public contact channels.

Support cannot buy control of the work.

Money, cloud credits, GPU access, equipment, data access, or other research resources are accepted only under terms that identify the provider, allowed use, rights, publication conditions, data access, and conflicts.

Support does not confer control over authorship, publication, research conclusions, clinical decisions, institutional representation, or access to restricted data.

Members remain responsible for the employment, institutional, public-service, military, ethics, data-use, privacy, and intellectual-property duties that apply to them.

The website does not collect form submissions.

The manuscript request form builds a draft in the visitor’s email application. Nothing is transmitted to or stored by this website before the visitor sends that email.

Information received

What an email may contain

Name, email, affiliation, role, professional profile, research interests, manuscript selection, and any other information the sender chooses to include.

Use

Responding to the inquiry

Inquiry information is used to review and respond to manuscript, research, membership, or collaboration requests and to maintain records when required.

Retention

Only as long as needed

Messages are retained only as needed to handle the inquiry or meet applicable governance, legal, contractual, research-compliance, security, or financial record duties.
