What a technical note contains

Enough information to inspect the result.

01

Data provenance

Dataset name or accession, cohort scope, assay, access conditions, inclusion criteria, and known batch or site effects.

02

Split definition

The unit held out for testing—sample, donor, site, or study—and why that split matches the stated generalization claim.

03

Method record

Model version, configuration, preprocessing, comparison methods, and code or reproducibility status.

04

Limits and corrections

Negative results, uncertainty, subgroup failures, leakage risks, changes to interpretation, and dated corrections.
