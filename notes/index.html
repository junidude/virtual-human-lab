Notes

Technical notes will record the data, methods, negative results, limits and corrections behind each project. None are public yet.

See the papers

What a note will contain

01

Data provenance

Dataset or accession, cohort, assay, access conditions, inclusion criteria and known batch or site effects.

02

Split definition

The unit held out for testing (sample, donor, site or study) and why it matches the claim.

03

Method record

Model version, configuration, preprocessing, comparison methods and reproducibility status.

04

Limits and corrections

Negative results, uncertainty, subgroup failures, leakage risks and dated corrections.
