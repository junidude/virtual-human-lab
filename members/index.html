Members and advisors

Researchers working across medicine, machine learning, and computational biology.

VHL brings clinical-domain expertise and model development together around a long-term question: how should a computational model represent and predict a person’s changing biology?

Active members participate in current VHL research. Pre-active members are preparing to join active project work. Advisors provide domain expertise without holding governance authority unless separately admitted as members.

Affiliation notice

Institutions are listed only to identify each person’s professional background. They do not sponsor, endorse, supervise, or assume responsibility for VHL unless a written agreement states otherwise.
