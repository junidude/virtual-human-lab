How VHL is organized.

Virtual Human Lab operates as an independent, non-commercial research association in Korea without corporate registration. It is not an official unit of a university, hospital, company, government agency, or military organization.

Representative: Seung Jun Lee · Decision body: General Assembly of Regular Members · Public page revised: August 13, 2026

The bylaws define voting membership, the General Assembly, officers, records, finance, conflicts, research standards, and dissolution.

Authority and continuity are defined in writing.

Membership changes do not dissolve VHL or transfer its records, accounts, domain, or assets.

Members

Regular Members vote

Regular Members form the General Assembly and vote on matters reserved by the bylaws.

Decisions

Major commitments need collective approval

Material funding, data access, intellectual property, debt, and institutional agreements require the approval specified in the bylaws.

Officers

Roles have limited authority

The Representative carries out resolutions and official communications within the authority granted by the bylaws.

Advisors

Advice does not confer a vote

Advisors and project collaborators do not vote or represent VHL unless separately admitted as Regular Members.

Records

Material decisions leave a record

Meeting records include the agenda, attendance, votes, recusals, and resolutions needed to document the decision.

Assets

No distribution to members

Funds and assets are used for academic purposes and are not distributed to members, officers, advisors, or related persons.

Representative · Seung Jun Lee

Seung Jun Lee is the VHL Representative under the bylaws. The role includes carrying out General Assembly resolutions, managing official communications, and representing VHL within the authority granted by those resolutions and rules.

No officer may present a member’s employer, hospital, university, company, government agency, or military organization as a VHL sponsor without written authorization.

Conflicts, support, and dissolution.

These rules are intended to preserve independent decisions and a reviewable record.

Conflicts

Disclosure and recusal

A member with a direct financial, employment, authorship, intellectual-property, disciplinary, or related conflict discloses it and does not vote on that matter.

Support

Terms documented in advance

Money, cloud credits, GPU access, equipment, and data access require written terms that address use, rights, publication, and compliance.

Independence

No purchase of scientific control

Support does not confer control over authorship, research conclusions, publication, clinical decisions, or institutional representation.

Records

Private material stays private

Signed originals, full minutes, personal records, accounts, protected research material, and institutional approval records are not posted publicly.

Dissolution

Liabilities and records come first

Dissolution requires the approval defined in the bylaws, followed by lawful handling of debts, contracts, data, accounts, and retained records.

Residual assets

Public-interest destination

After liabilities are settled, remaining assets go to an eligible non-profit academic, scientific, medical-education, or public-interest organization.
