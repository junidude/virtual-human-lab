Before requesting

Professional context is enough.

The request asks for your name, email, affiliation, role or title, an optional professional profile, the manuscript, and a short research context. Submitting the form opens a draft in your email application; the website does not transmit or store the form.

Do not include patient information, controlled-access data, non-public institutional information, or confidential material.

If the form or your email application does not open, write directly to junidude14@gmail.com.

Clinical scope

VHL is a research group, not a medical provider. Its manuscripts and research materials do not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or patient-specific medical advice.
